ThumpCut — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 August 2026

ThumpCut collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no server of ours that receives anything from your phone.

Your photos, videos and music stay on your phone

ThumpCut reads the photos, videos and songs you pick in order to build your reel, and it does all of that work on the phone itself. No photo, video or song — and nothing derived from them, such as beat timings — ever leaves your device. The finished reel is saved to your own gallery and shared only when you choose to share it, through apps you choose.

What the app fetches from the internet

  • The song list and its beat timings — downloaded from a public content delivery network. This is a plain download; nothing about you is sent with it beyond what any web request carries.
  • Song previews — when you preview a song from the built-in list, the audio streams from the provider's own public servers (for example Instagram's or Jamendo's content delivery networks), the same way a web browser would play it. Those providers see the same ordinary web request a browser would send.

If you only use your own music, the app works entirely offline after installation and fetches nothing at all.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Photos and videos — to show your gallery so you can pick items for a reel, and to save the finished reel.
  • Music / audio — to list the songs on your phone so you can pick one. The song is analysed on the device and never uploaded.

Children

ThumpCut is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at this address, and the "last updated" date above will move.
