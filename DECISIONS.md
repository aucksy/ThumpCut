2026-08-02 — **One clock for local music: the app renders an exact PCM WAV copy of the song at analysis time, and the preview plays and the export embeds THAT copy — never the original compressed file.** Owner-reported on device: cuts off the beat with his own MP3s while the built-in tracks were fine. Root cause: an MP3 has two clocks — the beat map's (an exact decode from time zero) and the player's (which may strip encoder padding, and lands only near a mid-file seek on VBR files) — and the built-in tracks are WAVs, which have one clock, which is why they never showed it. The copy is rendered from the same MediaCodec decode the analysis used, so the two clocks are identical by construction; it is cut off past the reel window, kept one-song-at-a-time, and re-rendered on demand when a cached analysis outlives it.
